# Recrafted: Alloys and Amulets

_A lightweight early/mid-game tech mod for Minecraft 1.7.10. Alloy tools, fuel-powered machines and gem amulets. No electricity needed. (yet)_

_Download the mod from [CurseForge](https://legacy.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/recrafted-alloys-amulets)._

![Minecraft 1.7.10](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-1.7.10-green) ![Forge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Forge-10.13.4.1614-orange) ![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Work%20in%20Progress-yellow)
***

> ⚠️ **Work in progress.** Content is added gradually and things may change or break between versions.

## ✨ Features

*   **Alloy tools and armor**: copper, tin, bronze, steel and more, each with its own stats.
*   **Fuel-powered machines**: process your materials with coal, charcoal and other fuels. No electricity, no wires.
*   **Gem amulets**: wearable amulets with special effects made from gems.
*   **Lightweight**: focused content that fits early/mid-game progression without bloating your modpack.

## 📦 Installation

1.  Install Minecraft 1.7.10 with Forge.
    
2.  Download the latest `.jar` from the [Releases](https://legacy.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/recrafted-alloys-amulets/files) page.
    
3.  Drop it into your `mods` folder.
    
4.  Launch the game.
